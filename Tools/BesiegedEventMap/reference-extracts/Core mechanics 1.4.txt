186: Mending the Schism
187:  For years there have been attempts to mend the Great Schism between the Rome and Constantinople. Both churches proclaim themselves to be the rightful inheritors of the temple of Jesus on Earth. Cardinal Isidore of Kiev, one of the most prominent priests of our Empire, has worked for many years to secure so called "Union of Florence" between Constantinople and Rome and he strongly advocates that we accept it in order so that we can secure aid from the Pope and other Catholic nations. Meanwhile Loukas Notaras, our chief minister, stresses that the people and most clergy are opposed to this, as accursed Latins have once already been the reason of our downfall, when they took the City and almost destroyed the Empire in 1204. We need to consider the political and religious implications, should we stick with our tradition or consider converting to the Catholic faith, hoping to secure additional assistance?
