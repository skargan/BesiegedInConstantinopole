BESIEGED — THE ROOMS BEHIND THE THRONE
Fully authored narrative package. Not yet integrated into Unity.
Three six-chapter relationship routes, plus four new chapters completing the existing scandal route.

romHelena1 — A Map with No Walls
Helena has spread a sea chart across a table intended for petitions. The islands are weighted with silver spoons. “Every map in this palace ends at the walls,” she says. “That is a habit of mind, not a fact of geography.” She is a widow of thirty-two, accustomed to negotiating with captains who mistake courtesy for agreement. She moves a spoon away from Constantinople. “You may need to know what lies beyond your duty.”

Ask her to explain the routes.
She draws you a passage through the islands. For an hour you speak as two people who might have futures, rather than an emperor and his petitioner.
Effects / conditions: {"trust:helena": 2, "flag:helenaRoutes": true}

Tell her survival begins with holding the City.
“Then hold it,” she answers, “but do not pretend there is nowhere else to stand.” She leaves the chart open.
Effects / conditions: {"trust:helena": 0}

Decline further private audiences.
She gathers the spoons before the chart. “Of course, Majesty. The sea will remain where it is.”
Effects / conditions: {"close:helena": "declined"}

Pay 400 gold to secure passage for the servants.

romHelena3 — The Captain Wants His Price

Helena has not paid him. “I can make the arrangement. I will not make your decision for you.” Beyond her composure is something less easily negotiated: she wants you to remain alive.

Reserve the passage for 1,000 gold.

romAnna1 — The Lamp in the Infirmary
Anna Synadene is washing a bowl when you enter the infirmary. A devout laywoman of twenty-nine, she has organised linen, meals and volunteers with a competence that makes the palace inventories look theatrical. She finishes with the bowl before greeting you. “If you have come for a blessing, Majesty, the priest is upstairs. If you have come to help, hold this.”

Help her carry water and speak with the wounded.
She gives you work before conversation. By the time you leave, she has stopped addressing every sentence to the crown.
Effects / conditions: {"trust:anna": 2}

Send 100 provisions to the infirmary.
She checks the delivery herself. Gratitude is written in a ledger beside the number of mouths it will feed.
Effects / conditions: {"iProvisions": -100, "trust:anna": 2, "flag:annaInfirmary": true}

romAnna2 — Mercy Has an Inventory

Anna has already stopped the bleeding. “I will not pretend the bed is empty,” she says. “Nor will I pretend your soldier's cousin is an abstraction.” She asks for resources to treat both, knowing exactly how scarce they are.

Provide 150 provisions and treat both men.
