1. EVENT PACING
Current RestUI builds one large recurring pool, then optionally overrides it with Galata/court content at25%. New recurring content therefore changes encounter frequency without an explicit design decision. Several due queues also precede fixed events. Do not allow backlog to silently skip a dated milestone.

Proposed selection order:
A. Campaign ending/critical immediate consequence, retaining its parent choice.
B. Dated milestones; never silently replace them. Queue other content.
C. Due continuations, ordered by due date then stable ID, subject to eligibility. An obsolete continuation resolves/cancels with a journal explanation. No fresh random roll to allow a promised continuation to occur.
D. On days without A/B/C: choose CATEGORY, then choose an eligible event within it. Provisional weights: unseen standalone one-offs45, new chain openings20, recurring incidents30, quiet breathing-space5. Empty categories redistribute proportionally. Never add a duplicate fallback into multiple categories.
E. In a category, favour never-seen recurring incidents over repeats3:1. Once-only events leave the pool permanently once resolved, including declined opportunities. Recurring cooldown>=30calendar days.
F. Maximum2active multi-part chains by default; the three romance acquaintances count as ONE relationship storyline for pacing, not three competing emergencies. Do not stop already-due critical continuations because capacity is full. Don't open another chain when existing due scenes are more than3days overdue. Initial romance meetings become eligible from siege day7.
G. Bound backlog: on two consecutive days occupied by noncritical continuations, prefer an eligible standalone next day unless a continuation has waited3days. Fixed/critical events always take priority. This rule needs the balance agent's simulations before final tuning.

Probability honesty: vary results and context, not the rules after the player chooses. Roll each outcome once, preserve it in the event checkpoint, and log the outcome. Conditional options should appear because of known focus, resources, relationships or intelligence. Show unavailable options with a reason where useful. Do not randomly remove sensible choices merely to make the player guess.

240–300events is a content goal, not a claim about current playable content. Report separate counts: unique encounters, chain chapters, mutually exclusive variants, and recurring templates. A250node catalogue with many exclusive branches cannot fill250days in a single campaign. Test actual campaign coverage/longest repetition gap, not catalogue size alone.
