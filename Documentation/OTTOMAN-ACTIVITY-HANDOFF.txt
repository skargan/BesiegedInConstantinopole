Presentation
- Full original painted background below the resource bar, compact right-hand navy/gold report panel.
- Three illustrations: quiet watch and tower construction; assault over the filled moat; Varangian repelling a Janissary on the battlements. The last depicts an intact opponent, not bisection.

- Calm tower site and both combatants were repositioned in generation so they remain visible beside the panel.
- Report text scrolls independently when long; work status and action remain visible.
- Action is Continue on non-assault days and Prepare the defence on assault days. Existing button listeners and simulation routes are preserved.

Read-only values
- fSiegeRamp: completion, clamped 0–100%.
- bSiegeTower / bBatteringRam / iOttArrowScreens: Ready or Not built. These systems do not currently track partial construction; no artificial intermediate percentages were added.
- iOttHornWorks: overland slipway completion.
- iOttChainDamage: damage inflicted on the anchorage.
- fMoat: remaining moat protection, explicitly labelled so it cannot be mistaken for Ottoman construction completion.
Completed/ready works stay listed. Destruction or clearing follows existing state; this is not a historical ledger of previously destroyed engines.
