MECHANICS REVIEW QUEUE — NOT SILENTLY IMPLEMENTED

Priority | Finding | Current behaviour | Suggested next decision

1 | Preparation research can exhaust the technology catalogue.
The current starting roster has 100 monks. At the approved 1 progress/monk/day and
100 progress/Secret, assigning all 100 generates 1 Secret/day. Fourteen technologies
cost 33 Secrets in total. Even 10 new monks (2,000 gold) produce 33 Secrets in 330 research
days, within preparation. By comparison, camp intelligence costs 1,000 gold for a
risky immediate 0–2 Secrets. Research also produces Secrets spendable on espionage.
Keep approved rates for this playtest. Ask the balance agent to test research-first
campaigns. Prefer adjusting one clear research rate or preparation opportunity
cost after evidence, rather than adding another currency or hidden diminishing
returns. No costs, technology prices or spy rewards were changed in this patch.

2 | Ship repairs need a dependable source of damaged hulls.
The active defeated-fleet event creates survivors and sunk ships. The existing
retreat helper can create damaged ships, but currently has no active Yarn caller.
Repair queues now work, but damage generation deserves an explicit rule. Proposed:
divide non-returning hulls into damaged recoverable hulls and actual sinkings,
with the ratio informed by mission and battle result. Not implemented here.

3 | Automatic repair yards overlap with paid repairs.
Queued hulls are protected from duplicate repair. The older preparation benefit
still repairs otherwise unreserved hulls automatically. Review whether it should
instead shorten paid repairs or waive a limited number of commissions. Preserve
the player's existing preparation reward until that replacement is approved.

4 | Crusade prose and timing disagree.
Aggregate crusading volunteer choices describe arrivals over months but grant
their large packages immediately. Named state packages now share canonical aid
state; broad crusade volunteers remain separate. Future work should schedule
those arrivals in dated waves, then check total troops and food against pacing.

5 | Morean resources are not yet finite across dynastic and ordinary aid.
Some story choices recall the whole garrison/every able-bodied man, while the
small new Morean country package can later be renewed. Reconcile this through the
brothers' cooperation story and remembered commitments. Do not add another
resource meter unless it earns its interface space.

6 | Foreign treaty consequences need enacted choices to attach to.
Saved once-only relationship APIs exist, but trade privileges, dynastic marriage,
honoured/broken signed agreements and foreign merchant confiscation need concrete
authored events. Do not treat domestic disputes as foreign treaty breaches.
Galata remains a local political relationship. Its seizure should eventually
apply an explicit, visible Genoa penalty and Venice benefit, with approved values.

7 | Specialist recruitment is deliberately asymmetric.
Engineer/envoy/monk recruitment is a timed paid commission. Recruiting a camp
agent remains the existing risky intelligence major action and can raise camp
alert. This is now visible before confirmation. Consider a later distinction
between recruiting a home-trained agent and buying an enemy-camp contact.

8 | Off-map diplomacy is not absent diplomacy.
Crimea/Mamluks now have country panels and modest aid. They have no generic war
button or invented negotiated intervention chain. Ragusa, Ionian lordships and
Theodoro remain planned additions. The map's Turkmen umbrella colour still routes
the current embassy specifically to AqQoyunlu, not a fictional united government.

Retained assumption pending preference:50 gold per repair engineer is the base
price; Notaras's existing 5%/15% labour discount remains applicable.
