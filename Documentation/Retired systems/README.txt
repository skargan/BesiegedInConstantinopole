Version 36.8 — Society and court simplification

Current design authority: the designer explicitly retired political factions after playtesting Version36.7. The newer instruction supersedes earlier faction-development approvals.

Active society measures: army morale, public morale, noble support, clergy support, and Galata relations. Existing civic audiences and their costs are retained. Galata remains a special neighbouring civic relationship.

Retired: political influence, drift, passive bonuses, weekly favours, debts/demands, faction banishment and character allegiance gating; merchant favour; persistent fencing progression; the palace's preselected next-assault leadership and duplicated garden/rest controls. Palace map opens wall defence. General statistics no longer show mentorship; Apprentice Gunner remains a self-contained event with its existing five-day outcome. The merchant levy remains independent income.

Compatibility: legacy faction APIs are inert; old faction keys are neutralised on load. A saved political demand has a safe Continue route. Historical resources, deaths/departures and already queued recruitment dates are not reversed. Old fencing values cannot improve combat, and queued wall leadership cannot auto-select frontline. Explicit assault decisions and sword minigames remain.

Siege overview now displays the current commander's benefit and Change commander. Review and confirmation precede appointment. The final confirmation retains the existing turn cost and hourglass language.

Future reference: original implementations and event excerpts are stored beside this file. Reintroduction requires a new design decision, a clearly useful player choice and migration/testing. Do not silently reactivate archived systems. A future merchant system needs real consequences before returning to the public ledger.
