VERSION 33 — WAR BEYOND THE WALLS

Implemented
Interactive painted geographical map, independent polygon overlays, gold hover outlines, zoom/pan, historical reference atlas, Constantinople-to-Palace shortcut, province intelligence entry points, transparent status-symbol atlas.
Three Ottoman personalities: Aggressive, Cautious, Balanced. Legacy Rash saves map to Aggressive. Four siege strategies remain unchanged.
One provincial agent assignment uses the existing travel, ledger and depot-disruption rules. Destination persists through saves. Viewing the map consumes no campaign randomness.

Cartography revision
Eleven Ottoman gameplay districts: Thrace; Moesia; Macedonia & Thessaly; Bithynia; Opsikion; Thrakesion (including Lydia); Cibyrrhaeot Coast; Phrygia; Anatolikon; Armeniakon; Paphlagonia.
Lydia merged into Thrakesion. The former Anatolikon–Armeniakon region split into two. Save indices are append-only: legacy Lydia assignments and ledgers resolve to Thrakesion; existing Anatolia assignments remain Anatolikon; Armeniakon receives a new slot.
Opsikion restricted to the Asian shore of Marmara. Coast-facing polygons traced from the displayed artwork, including Karaman's southern coast and northern Anatolian coastal segments. By explicit design decision, Candar/Paphlagonia is treated as fully Ottoman-controlled in this gameplay version. Historically independent in 1453, it is deliberately simplified here; revise its extent and status together if a later design needs it. It uses the same province selection, authority and spy-operation rules as other Ottoman districts. Its save slot is appended, preserving existing assignments.
Constantinople placed at the European Bosporus entrance. Morea follows the Peloponnese coastline. Genoese Gazaria represents the southern Crimean coastal belt. Island outlines include Euboea and Venetian/Genoese associated possessions. Mount Athos is not marked.
Names follow a Byzantine perspective; these are composite gameplay districts, not claims to exact Byzantine theme or Ottoman administrative boundaries. The painted map is stylised and is not a GIS projection. Inland frontiers remain approximate.

Art
Generated with the built-in image generator: unlabeled geographical relief from Italy to western Caspian, southern Ukraine to below Crete; ochre/olive terrain, blue-teal seas, restrained painted aesthetic. Separate transparent 4x4 atlas contains agent, network, revolt, imperial control, land/naval battle, army, galley, grain, cannon, garrison, treasury, burning depot, messenger, broken chain and order symbols. Imported without texture compression, with mipmaps and trilinear filtering. Original generated files retained.

Scope boundary
Map selection and provincial intelligence are functional. Production roles are descriptive. Control/rebellion/war markers are prepared state readers, not a completed regional economic or foreign-war simulation. No fabricated battle strength is displayed.
