Open Military and recruitment > Specialists > Infirmary and Monasteries.
The Technology screen opens the same service through its Monastic Research summary.

Rates are editable in Assets/Resources/Content/MonasticResearch.json. Research
operates during preparation as well as siege. Screen visits and real time do not
produce knowledge. Existing saves begin with all monks in the infirmary and no
retroactive research. Pending choices, progress and processed dates are saved.

ENGINEERS AND CREWS
Order               Gold        Materials   Engineers              Ordinary manpower   Time
Engineer            250         —           1 instructor           1                   10 days
Envoy               750         —           none                   1                   10 days
Five monks          1,000       —           none                   existing rule       5 days
Cannon              existing    10          2; one stays aboard    4                   10 days
Warship             existing    30          5; one stays aboard    49                  30 days
Repair damaged ship 250         5           2; both return         10 replacements     5 days

Total cannon crew is five and total ship crew is fifty, including the permanent
engineer. Ordinary crew is reserved at construction confirmation. Existing ships,
cannons and old unfinished orders are grandfathered; loading never creates a
retroactive manpower bill. Foreign crewed gifts do not consume local recruits.
Sunk ships do not return their fifty crew to the recruitment pool. Damage and
withdrawal are distinct from sinking. A hull already in a repair queue is excluded
from the older automatic repair-yard benefit.

When no engineer is available, one trainee may learn without an instructor at
normal price and duration. Only one such order can be active, including when the
other engineers are committed to other jobs. Availability is shared across jobs.

LAND-WALL REPAIRS
Assign N available engineers, normally 1–10. Advanced Masonry raises this to 20.
Gold: 50 N base, with Notaras's existing labour discount preserved.
Materials: outer 3 N; middle 2 N; innerN.
Restoration: one uniform integer roll from N to floor(1.5 N) percentage points,
capped at 100% integrity. The preview shows the capped range and affordability.
Repair occurs before the following attack. One separate task casualty roll:
95% none; 2% one killed; 3% one wounded. Surviving engineers return onD+2;
wounded engineers enter medical care instead. Restoration is not reduced after
the casualty roll. Sea-wall repairs remain a separate action/rule.
Advanced Masonry retains the RomanConcrete save ID and its separate old sea-wall
material saving. Its former land-wall material discount is replaced by team size.

DIPLOMACY
Twenty-five stable country records now share the country screen and map routes.
Three envoys begin available. Dispatch, residence and recall use calendar dates.
New envoys may be trained. One envoy cannot occupy two courts or work en route.

Attitude     Daily goodwill while resident
Hostile      +0.10
Wary         +0.25
Neutral      +0.50
Friendly     +1.00
Allied       +1.50

Goodwill and attitude are separate. Gifts cost 3,000 gold and grant 10 goodwill;
their 50% attitude improvement can reach Friendly, never create Allied status.
The displayed final acceptance chance is the exact value rolled, capped at 95%.
Approved Church Union modifiers are ongoing chance modifiers, not permanent
changes to base goodwill. Repudiation penalties expire after 30 calendar days.

Action             Requirement                         Result
Initial aid        1,000 gold; no resident needed        Existing cargo/arrival rules
Renew aid appeal   Resident; prior aid resolved         1,000 gold +10 resident days
Request war        Resident; Friendly; chance at least 75%; eligible court
                                                       Success: Allied, then mobilisation
Renew war appeal   Failed previous war request          Separate retry permission

One renewal project per court at a time. Each permission is consumed once;
recalling an envoy pauses resident work. Sea transport vessels return home.
Only explicitly donated warships join the fleet. Named Papal/Hospitaller story
packages share their country's initial aid entitlement, preventing duplicate aid.

Regional intervention delays a finite number of actual Ottoman shipments after
mobilisation. It does not invent armies, battles or casualties. Shipment budgets,
target provinces and delays are editable in DiplomaticPowers.json. A parcel
already stopped by rebellion/liberation is not disrupted again by an ally.
Major held defences and major naval victories have bounded, saved goodwill rewards.
