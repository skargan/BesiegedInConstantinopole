CURRENT DESKTOP RELEASE — 21 October 2026, Midnight Mosaic 17

Launch: desktop Besieged.lnk -> Play-Besieged.cmd -> Builds/Windows-MidnightMosaic/Besieged.exe.
The old launcher targeted Windows-Espionage. Historical build folders are retained as snapshots, not updated copies. This directory is a ZIP checkout without .git; no GitHub publication was performed.
Default ConfigurePlaytest.Build output now uses Windows-MidnightMosaic; legacy flags retain historical destinations. BuildLabel is shared by menu and HUD.

Training: five documented tiers retained; 5000 gold / whole troop type / one major action; five calendar days; troops remain available. Save stores deadline and target quality. Training completes once. Preparation turns may advance beyond the deadline in one turn.
Roman Tactics, Upgraded Crossbows, Cataphract Armour each add 25% of trained lethality. Grenades +0.25 and Handcannons +0.5 remain. Cavalry contributes half total lethality at walls; Dismounted Champions restores full contribution. Varangian Drills +20%; new specialist technologies cost 2 secrets each. Artillery Ships doubles the defensive fleet report from 100 to 200 enemy casualties. It does not invent a per-ship wall-assault coefficient. Existing technology keys remain for save compatibility.

Surrender: seven rows, adding nobility to the six requested categories. Offer pressure = 40% fury + 20% camp alert + 40% active-wall damage. Bands below 25, 50, 75 and 75+ select adverse terms. Mid-range offers may sacrifice Emperor/household while sparing inhabitants. Extreme fury plus almost-destroyed walls can produce execution of inhabitants; high alert can extend execution to household. These are authored gameplay rules, not reconstructions of actual Ottoman terms.
Offers are fixed until the date changes, saved when requested, with a separate final acceptance. Enslavement/execution adds assassination risk: inhabitants 10, military 50, household 20 percentage points; sum max80. One roll at acceptance, result saved. Assassination repudiates surrender and ends the player's reign; the City continues resisting in ending text. No playable successor campaign yet.
Ten achievement entries: survival, surrender assassination and negotiated surrender track completion; seven planned entries are visibly marked. Test saves never unlock user achievements.
