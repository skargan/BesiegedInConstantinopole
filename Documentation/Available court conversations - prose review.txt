AVAILABLE COURT CONVERSATIONS — PROSE REVIEW
5 November 2026

Revised the nine core speakers who can serve in the active court: Giustiniani,
Diedo, Notaras, Orhan, Sphrantzes, Orban, Grant, Isidore and Kjold. The approved
Raimondina material was used as a tone reference and remains unchanged.

Introductions now combine the person's work with a private concern or a specific
impression of Constantinople. Familiar servants no longer introduce themselves
to the Emperor as though applying for a position. The aim is a conversation:
plain spoken detail, occasional irritation or humour, and less polished maxim.

The personal questions retain their three replies and recorded response slots.
Giustiniani wants a clear chain of command; Diedo wants his sailors remembered;
Notaras worries about protecting his household; Orhan asks what survival would
mean for his freedom; Sphrantzes objects to the handling of unwelcome news;
Orban wants permission to condemn an unsafe gun; Grant needs support for work
whose success is invisible; Isidore faces consciences left unsettled by policy;
Kjold considers what to tell younger guards about courage and their inheritance.

Siege assessment now acknowledges four identifiable reports: a repulsed assault,
an abandoned defensive line, an explicit operational delay and enemy resupply.
Each core speaker has a response in his own voice. Other reports remain visible,
followed by an explicitly wider assessment; an unrelated practical suggestion
is no longer presented as an explanation of the reported event. Preparation
responses also distinguish these nine speakers.

No new promises with resource effects, rewards, quests, allegiance changes,
availability rules or unlock gates were added. The existing food/wall checks in
advice remain. Visitors 100–105 and their conversations were left untouched.
The unused Personal() material was preserved for future work. There is still
no separate city-conversation button; city detail belongs to the available
introduction instead. Conversation labels in CouncilGardenUI were not changed.

Verification: all 15 existing question records still have three replies and
three corresponding responses. CharacterQuestions has identical non-string
code. Availability/identity code and all visitor passages compare unchanged
against the pre-edit copies. These are structural checks, not a Unity playtest.
The longest revised completed question exchange is 591 characters before UI
headings. Review the final exported layout with the current conversation UI.
