BESIEGED - DESIGN DECISIONS, 12 OCTOBER 2026
Status: agreed direction and implementation requirements; not a claim of completed features.

Confirmed by the designer
- Endurance victory requires surviving 240 siege days. Preparation events do not count.
- Add a separate achievement for exceeding historical survival.
- Provide accurate city information and substantial enemy information. Intelligence about enemy intentions improves with spies in the enemy camp.
- Resource collapse creates severe, escalating but potentially recoverable crises, rather than automatically ending a run.
- Construction duration varies by project. Emergency repairs can have immediate effects.
- Several conditional event paths can lead to Mehmed's death, ending the siege. No permanent assassination button. Paths may involve spies, character branches or opportunities arising after prolonged resistance without spies.
- Defeat should feel good but unsatisfying: recognise specific exceptional accomplishments and leave the player wanting to try again.

Historical basis and proposed counting convention
- Oxford Cabinet and Koc University library references date the siege from 6 April to 29 May 1453.
- Sources: https://www.cabinet.ox.ac.uk/fall-constantinople-1453 and https://libguides.ku.edu.tr/byzantineconstantinople/fallofconstantinople-1453
- Those dates are 53 elapsed days apart, spanning 54 calendar dates when counted inclusively. Earlier investment dates also appear in histories; use an explicit convention.
- Proposed achievement: reach 30 May with the city still holding. With 6 April as displayed siege day 1, that is the start of day 55, after 54 completed daily turns. This avoids equating a day just started with a day survived.
- Endurance victory must check 240 completed siege days, not merely arrival at displayed day 240.

Proposed ending-screen design, awaiting content and implementation
- Record actual campaign milestones as they happen, preserving them across save/load.
- Display outcome and immediate cause, completed siege days, historical comparison, and up to three distinctive accomplishments grounded in recorded events.
- Example categories: exceptional defence, recovery from a crisis, successful relief, people rescued, and character outcomes. Only show categories the game actually measures; do not infer people saved from population alone.
- Include a short factual account of the collapse and one useful clue grounded in known information. Do not invent an inevitable alternative victory or reveal hidden event branches.

Verified implementation gaps on 12 October
- CampaignHUD currently ends campaigns immediately at zero faction reputation; this conflicts with the newly approved crisis model.
- SiegeDay is a one-based current-day counter; using it directly for days survived and the 240-day victory requires correction and boundary tests.
- Existing date calculation needs alignment with the agreed siege start.
- This document records decisions only. Crisis chains, richer endings, historical achievement and assassination branches are not implemented by this update.

Earlier decisions still apply
- Recruitment remains unlimited by action count until playtesting; resource costs still apply.
- One major action plus minor actions is provisional. Do not introduce a three-minor-action cap without further discussion.
- Common sense takes priority, then the mechanics document, then existing code; prefer historical plausibility between equally good alternatives.
- Rarity estimates for victory express intended difficulty, not predetermined win probabilities.

Implemented later in the UI update
- Siege date now begins on 6 April; CompletedSiegeDays counts finished days and the 240-day ending uses that count. Day-one quick-start date is covered by the gameplay checks.
- Faith and Unity wartime compact is implemented; persuasion alternative was declined. Isidore is retained by partial Union and compact, lost through explicit exile.
