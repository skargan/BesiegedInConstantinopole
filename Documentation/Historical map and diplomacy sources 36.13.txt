BESIEGED 36.13 - HISTORICAL MAP AND DIPLOMACY CHECK
2 November 2026. Focus: a readable schematic map of the opening 1452-53 campaign.

Implementation guidance

Ragusa is a separate republic, not a Venetian possession. Its diplomatic value
lies in commerce, discreet supplies and financial networks. Independent actions
are appropriate; a generic field-army war option is not required.
Source: Lovorka Coralic, Croatian Institute of History, The Ragusans in Venice
from the Thirteenth to the Eighteenth Century, Dubrovnik Annals 3 (1999), pp.13-40.
https://hrcak.srce.hr/file/12664

The Tocco Ionian lordships must be distinguished from Venetian Corfu and Paxos.
Cephalonia, Zakynthos, Ithaca and Leucas/Lefkada form the practical island group;
do not give their diplomatic court all the Ionian Islands. Their mainland
possessions had contracted, so avoid painting all Epirus as a Tocco state.
Source: John Freely, The Ionian Islands, historical opening chapter (publisher
preview); official Kefalonia tourism historical booklet corroborates the sequence.
https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9780857718280_A23726065/preview-9780857718280_A23726065.pdf
https://visitkefaloniaisland.gr/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/%CE%91-STROLL-DOWN-HIST.-KEFALONIA.pdf

Naples, Sicily and Sardinia share Alfonso's sovereign in this period. Use distinct
possession labels but the existing Naples diplomatic state, avoiding an additional
Aragon aid allowance. Alfonso inherited Sardinia and Sicily in 1416 and conquered
Naples in 1442; their different succession belongs to 1458, after this campaign.
Source: Istituto della Enciclopedia Italiana, Alfonso V, Dizionario di Storia.
https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/alfonso-v_%28Dizionario-di-Storia%29/

Corsica belongs in the Genoese diplomatic grouping, with local resistance and
uneven authority acknowledged in its note. Genoa's administration was transferred
to the Banco di San Giorgio in 1453; a solid colour is a gameplay simplification,
not proof of uniform direct control over every interior lordship.
Source: scholarly research on fifteenth-century Corsican ports and navigation;
KU Leuven research project on the Banco's sovereign jurisdiction.
https://www.persee.fr/doc/acths_1764-7355_2008_act_128_3_1325?pageId=T2_14
https://research.kuleuven.be/portal/en/project/3H120583

Milan is a Sforza duchy by 1452, not the defunct Ambrosian Republic. Florence and
Siena remain separate republics. Florence has maritime access through Pisa and
Livorno; Siena through its Maremma possessions and Talamone. Central Italian
Papal territory opens the existing Papal embassy. Small lordships, republics and
local papal vicariates should not be described as direct uniform administration.
Sources: Comune di Milano, Castello Sforzesco; Italian national archives' Livorno
record; Treccani, Talamone.
https://www2.comune.milano.it/web/castello-sforzesco/scopri-il-castello/la-storia-del-castello/il-castello-dei-visconti-e-degli-sforza
https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/livorno/
https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/talamone_%28Enciclopedia-Dantesca%29/

Lithuania and Poland are distinct realms under Casimir, not the later Commonwealth.
Separate map labels can open the existing shared embassy without duplicating its
aid. The visible Lithuanian frontier should reach the northern Black Sea roughly
between the Dniester and lower Dnieper; do not extend it over Crimea or the entire
Azov coast. Control of the steppe was fluid. Depicting a precise hard line for 1452
is an inference, not a documented survey. The map note should call this a frontier.
Sources: Visuotine lietuviu enciklopedija; Darius Baronas, Lithuanian Institute of
History, critical analysis/edition of the 1427 letter describing coastal castles;
Encyclopedia of Ukraine, Vytautas and Black Sea entries. The latter describes
mid-fifteenth-century erosion of Lithuanian gains; VLE places the loss across the
later fifteenth century. These do not establish a precise 1452 border.
https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/lietuvos-didzioji-kunigaikstyste/
https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/lietuvos-didzioji-kunigaikstyste-nuo-krevos-sutarties-iki-liublino-unijos-14-a-pabaiga-1569-institucines-monarchijos-laikotarpis/
https://open.istorija.lt/journal/IST/article/750/info
https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pagesvyvytautasthegreat.htm
https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CB%5CL%5CBlackSea.htm

Crimea must have three political identities: the Giray Khanate in the peninsula's
interior/steppe, Genoese Gazaria's coastal colonies, and the Orthodox principality
of Theodoro around Mangup in the south-west. The Khanate is not an Ottoman
dependency at the campaign's opening. Theodoro survived until the Ottoman siege
of 1475. Do not paint the entire south coast as Theodoro or all Crimea as Genoese.
Sources: Encyclopedia of Ukraine, Giray dynasty; Treccani, Caffa; archaeological
conference findings on the Mangup palace; CEU dissertation discussing documentary
and inscriptional evidence for medieval Crimean Gothia/Theodoro.
https://www.encyclopediaofukraine.com/display.asp?linkpath=pages%5CG%5CI%5CGireidynasty.htm
https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/caffa_%28Enciclopedia-Italiana%29/
https://old.chersonesos-sev.ru/wp-content/uploads/2015/10/ForInternet.pdf
https://www.etd.ceu.edu/2002/smirnov_oleksiy.pdf

The Mamluk Sultanate covers Egypt and Syria, with frontier dependencies distinct
from its directly governed core. The Adana/Cilician gap belongs to the Ramadanid
marches, not simply Karaman or the eastern Turkmen confederations. Dulkadir around
Maras/Elbistan is another buffer principality. A fast schematic map may group
these marches with the Mamluk diplomatic sphere, provided their labels and notes
explicitly retain local rulers and qualified suzerainty. That grouping is a game
interface choice, not a claim that their rulers had no independent politics.
Sources: TDV Islam Ansiklopedisi, Faruk Sumer on Ramazanogullari and Refet Yinanc
on Dulkadirogullari. Tarsus repeatedly changed hands; exact local lines remain
schematic. Avoid an invented 1452 cadastral border.
https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/ramazanogullari
https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/dulkadirogullari

Boundary confidence
High: identities, principal islands, coasts, shared rulers and separate embassies.
Medium: broad regional coverage of the principal courts.
Low: exact inland frontiers, northern steppe control and minor Italian lordships.
The background is generated geographical artwork; polygons are a legible campaign
overlay, not a historical atlas. Keep the separate historical reference map.

All aid, travel, goodwill and intervention magnitudes remain editable game-balance
choices; these sources validate historical framing, not numerical game effects.

HERALDRY HANDOFF (for the main art developer; no assets changed here)

Lithuania: the mounted swordsman/Vytis is appropriate to the period. Avoid a flag
of the post-1569 Commonwealth as a claimed 1452 national flag. A shared Casimir
embassy can visually pair the Lithuanian rider and Polish eagle as distinct arms.
https://www.vle.lt/straipsnis/vytis/
https://www.lrs.lt/sip/getFile?guid=661a2fa0-634a-483e-9522-859b0e06c732

Mamluks: rank/patron blazons are well attested, including the cupbearer's cup and
secretary's pen box. They were not interchangeable national flags. The familiar
late tripartite emblem associated with Qaytbay became prominent after this siege.
Use a documented period-inspired court medallion as an artistic game identifier,
not a claim to reproduce an exact 1452 flag. Do not use the modern Egyptian flag.
https://exhibitions.kelsey.lsa.umich.edu/pearls/objects/blazon1.html
https://islamicart.museumwnf.org/database_item.php?id=object%3BISL%3Bsy%3BMus01_A%3B48%3Ben

Crimea: the Giray tarak-tamga is the appropriate dynastic motif. TDV records the
mark on Mengli's coins and a 1475 document; a later museum coin clearly illustrates
it. This supports the family motif, not the modern blue national flag in 1452.
https://islamansiklopedisi.org.tr/giray
https://museum.mincult.gov.ua/collections/325969

Tocco: blue/silver wavy horizontal bars are an appropriate family reference, but
armorial variants differ in number and later composite chiefs. Avoid presenting
a later elaborate coat with accumulated titles as an exact Leonardo III banner.
The armorial index cites Rietstap among its sources; this is a secondary reference,
not a verified contemporary artefact.
https://www.armoriale.it/wiki/Armoriale_delle_famiglie_italiane_%28Toc%29

Theodoro: a Byzantine double-headed eagle is a defensible court-inspired emblem;
exact flag colours/layout were not verified in this bounded check. Treat generated
art as interpretation. Further source lead: Pascal Androuidis (2017), Presence de
l'aigle bicephale en Trebizonde et dans la principaute grecque de Theodoro en Crimee
(XIVe-XVe siecles). Do not infer an exact national banner from a stone emblem.
