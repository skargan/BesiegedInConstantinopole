MAP OF THE CITY - INTEGRATION

Open Map of the City from the title menu to browse, or from the Imperial Council during a campaign to inspect and repair defences.

The supplied image is preserved unchanged. Four painted hover regions use gold outlines; the two western ramparts lead to the same Theodosian Wall inspector. The middle and eastern defensive lines lead to the Constantinian and Severan Wall inspectors. These labels map to the game's existing three defensive layers, rather than claiming this illustration is an archaeological reconstruction.

Campaign selection uses the existing DefenseActions inspector. Repair prices, improvement, affordability checks and major-action costs remain governed by the existing game rules. Browsing the title-menu map does not initialise or alter a campaign. Return leads back to the originating menu or map.
