BESIEGED - DESIGN PHILOSOPHY AND DELIVERY QUEUE
Designer direction consolidated 22 October 2026. Working decisions, not a claim of released features.

DELIVERY METHOD
A playable preview is the primary unit of progress. Freeze a small release scope, compile, exercise the changed player path, publish a numbered desktop folder, and ask for player feedback before expanding it. Do not wait for every idea in this thread. Separate known bugs, prototypes, implemented source, verified gameplay and published builds. Preserve the other agent's files through narrow shared-file edits. Keep one active implementation focus; batch independent reads and validations, not dependent mutations. New ideas enter this queue unless they unblock the release. Design planning should reduce rework, not become another deliverable delaying play.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
Fundamental campaign state and consequential choices first; presentation and optional interaction build on that state. Assess each feature as game designer (decision and consequence), UI designer (hierarchy, legibility, consistent controls), and UX designer (comprehension, feedback, recovery, pacing). Use short, readable, responsive challenges with clear battlefield cues and immediate feedback. Preserve historical material constraints and credible siege tactics. Decorative effects must not pretend to change outcomes. Do not invent expert consultation: record actual reviews and published design references separately from design judgement.

EVENT INFORMATION - APPROVED
Show exact player costs, committed troops and irreversible commitments before selection. Major early preparation decisions show exact guaranteed benefits. Later uncertain outcomes use specific narrative clues and qualitative scale where supported by the actual range; do not mechanically promise 'usually 1-2 agents'. State the uncertainty without exposing enemy secrets. Report actual visible changes afterwards, distinguishing a delta from the new total. Qualitative words must be consistent within each resource type; a 'serious loss' cannot mean the same fixed number across different armies. Do not hide a known certain cost inside flowery prose. Keep exact hidden distributions in developer notes, not ordinary event help. Audit content in batches after this preview rather than rewrite every event before delivery.
