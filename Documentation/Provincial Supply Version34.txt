VERSION 34 IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT — PROVINCIAL SUPPLY AND RESISTANCE
Approved: 25 October 2026

Finite siege budget: arrival food 90 army-days, later deliveries 150 army-days, total 240. Food remains an abstract army-day unit. All delivery paths must debit one ledger; no emergency creation when stores empty. Existing 15-day starvation refill and AI free cannon/ammunition/150-man reinforcement must be replaced together.
Troops: starting contingent is half the campaign maximum. Snapshot starting strength by troop type; schedule an equal reserve contingent. Casualties never enlarge reserves. Lost or blocked shipments do not create replacement troops.
Provisional artillery balancing proposal: 12 initial cannons, 10 later; 30 initial ammunition stocks and120 later. Each bombardment operation uses one stock under the existing rule; these are not individual cannonballs. Grand cannon remains separately tracked. These are provisional gameplay quantities, not claimed historical counts. Reinforcement parcels preserve the initial troop-type mix and sum to exactly one additional initial army.

Province food quotas (sum150): Thrace24; Moesia24; Macedonia24; Bithynia15; Opsikion10; Thrakesion12; Cibyrrhaeot Coast6; Phrygia10; Anatolikon10; Armeniakon9; Paphlagonia6.
Distribute over five delivery windows: siege days15–35,45–65,75–95,105–125,135–165. Generate dated shipments once using campaign randomness, persist them, and never reroll on load. Divide each province quota by five with integer remainder assigned to early windows. Initial stock absorbs gaps; disrupted provinces permanently lose their scheduled exports while rebellion/liberation persists. Only summaries and occasional convoy events are player-facing.

One agent per province. Travel preserves the existing seven-day baseline. Stationed agents toggle authority reduction or resistance organising, each1–2 points per calendar day. Passive organising is allowed at any authority. Clamp authority/resistance0–100. Updates use last-processed calendar date and must be idempotent across reloads.
Active actions: weaken authority always available (1–10 points); attack food convoys at authority<=85 (3–7 army-days from actual undelivered supply); finance resistance at authority<=70 (+1–10 points). Display cost before confirmation. Approved costs: weaken authority500 gold; convoy attack1,000; finance resistance1,000; each consumes a major action. Standing instructions are free.
Rebellion begins at authority<=45. Province stops exports. Each day authority and resistance independently lose1–5 points simultaneously. Resistance zero means Ottoman victory, including ties; otherwise authority zero means liberation. Resolve each rebellion exactly once.
Suppression: authority +10 elsewhere, +20 in neighbouring provinces (neighbour amount replaces global amount); agent dies; province cannot receive an agent for100 calendar days. Liberation: authority -5 elsewhere, -10 in neighbours (replacement); monthly irregular treasury gifts. Cascades must use a stable province snapshot and queue newly triggered rebellions, avoiding order-dependent processing. Liberated provinces cannot silently revert through global authority boosts.
Neighbours: explicit geographical adjacency data, never inferred from label proximity. Maritime neighbours require a separate design rule; default land adjacency only.

Required validation: food totals240; reserves equal initial contingent; no infinite reinforcement; simultaneous rebellion ties; threshold boundaries85/70/45; neighbouring modifiers apply once; exact100-day exclusion; independent agents; reload cannot repeat deliveries, passive work, battles or gifts; legacy one-cell migration; old campaigns do not receive a fresh90-day windfall.
Integration order: supply ledger and migration; provincial state and daily clock; operations interface; rebellion results and reports; AI budget integration; seeded balance runs; numbered playable release.

Previously paid in-flight operations complete through their old resolver before handing the agent to the new system. Fresh games receive the new opening budget; mid-siege saves retain current stores and omit already-past scheduled deliveries. Preparation sabotage is deducted from the new opening budget.
