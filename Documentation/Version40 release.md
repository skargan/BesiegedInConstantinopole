# Against Fate: Saving Constantinople — Version 40

## Mercenary brokers join the base game

Open **Diplomacy and court → Mercenary brokers**, or click Malta on the theatre map. Five monthly offers draw from twenty fictional companies inspired by contemporary military traditions. Each has a named captain, distinct portrait, troop package, price and departure port. All baseline voyages take 75 days or less.

Pay half at signing and half on arrival. Contracts persist in saves and remain visible in the ledger. Departures travel from Venice, Malta, Cyprus or Caffa on predefined sea routes. Hover a voyage for its cargo, remaining time and payments; click to open its contract. Troops merge into existing categories on arrival; warships include operational crews. Foreign recruits do not debit the city's domestic recruitment pool. All arrivals add their people once to the shared food population.

One saved roll at signing determines the voyage's incident and date. Incidents include storms, being blown off course, greedy captains, treachery, desertion, disputed command, an unexpected coastal engagement, evading patrols, an unequal sea battle and ambush in the Bosphorus. Recoverable incidents pause or delay the voyage; no second hazard roll follows. Decisions await your reply in the contracts tab and appear in mercenary dispatches.

Sea control now uses the approved three states: more than 50% numerical superiority gives dominance; exactly 50% remains contested. Weather, reliability, patrol and Bosphorus hazards occupy additive, mutually exclusive bands. Chartered passage has 2% lethal storm and 3% course delay; company flotillas have 1% lethal storm. Patrol risk totals 6% in contested seas and 12% under Ottoman dominance; closed Bosphorus adds 50 points for chartered passage or 15 for company flotillas.

The Moreote sanctuary offer provisionally costs 1,000 gold and −5 noble support; 150 dependants accompany its 300 combatants. The Rhodian detachment requires 60 Hospitaller goodwill and is blocked when its diplomatic contingent is already promised or delivered. Contracting it blocks an ordinary duplicate appeal. These are first-pass terms; other non-cash bargains remain expansion candidates within the base game.

Defectors add their land combatants to Ottoman infantry once. Their ships, specialists and cannons are not delivered to either side in this first pass. A camp agent sent to settle disputed command is permanently spent. Half the original price remains due after surcharges.

## Playtest limits

Prices, reliability and arrival times are provisional, not a claim of complete campaign balance. One charter per named company per campaign. Sea routes are illustrative. The captain atlas represents companies visually; no independent tactical formation simulation is added. Broader prize-rights, patronage and resource-payment bargains remain future additions. Historical company traditions inform the roster; the named captains and formations are fictional.

Existing desktop builds and saves are preserved. Keep separate saves for this version while testing. No Steam App ID or live Steam publishing is configured by this release.
