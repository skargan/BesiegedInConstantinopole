PLAYER EXPERIENCE REVIEW — COUNCIL r5

Verdict
The prototype has useful decisions but still needs a more coherent campaign arc. The player's task should be to recognise a threat, make a costly choice, observe its consequence and reconsider the plan. Navigation must not be the difficulty.

Implemented in this pass
- Council briefing: weakest of four internal support measures, nearest committed relief, secrets and ammunition; explains inspection versus the major action.
- Rest explicitly labelled as a major action.
- Visible scroll controls on long council screens and outcome reports.
- Spy buttons explain missing agents, payment or targets. Technology buttons explain secret requirements and where to obtain secrets.
- Pending technology effects no longer described as active just because an event sets their flags.
- Court returns to Palace; Escape respects open modal screens first.
- Campaign menu preserves an unresolved outcome and its Continue callback.
- Army morale label distinguished from public morale.
- Consistent gold button outlines and highlighted/pressed/disabled states.

Remaining design work, proposed for discussion
1. Preparation pacing: reconcile the approved monthly 1452 preparation direction with the existing event calendar; avoid simply compressing every authored event onto consecutive clicks.
2. Characters: give each a small specialist benefit and a stronger command appointment benefit, with explicit numbers and trade-offs. Present historical background separately from game effects.
3. Major-event promises: track obligations such as the apprentice's rescue and consequences of forged orders; proposed prose is not yet a working event chain.
4. Genoese politics: distinguish Giustiniani, Galata, Genoa and overseas interests. Conquest should resolve territorial control while creating diplomatic costs.
5. Negotiated endings: design the promised surrender milestones and explain why the terms improve. Keep personal survival distinct from the City's outcome.
6. Resource collapse and food: audit actual consumption and crisis rules before showing forecasts. Do not display a reassuring food-duration estimate without a verified consumption model.
7. Territorial map: attach events and intelligence to places before treating the supplied illustration as a functional strategic map.

Scope
No balance, randomness, campaign-duration or ending rules changed. No new illustrated atlas assets or territorial map integrated in this pass.

Validation
195 automated checks passed. Windows development build compiled. Visual inspection covers selected 1600x900 screens; no claim of exhaustive campaign balance or all-resolution QA.
