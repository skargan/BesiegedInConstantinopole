BESIEGED VERSION 36.11 - MONASTERIES AND EMBASSIES
2 November 2026

PLAY
Close any earlier game instance and open the desktop Besieged-Latest shortcut,
or run Besieged.exe in this Version-36.11 folder. Older published builds remain.
The newest archive with each filename is copied from earlier Version folders.

MONASTIC RESEARCH
Open Military and recruitment > Specialists > the monk/infirmary entry, or
Technologies > Monastic Research. Both open Infirmary and Monasteries.
The existing infirmary is at the top; scroll down to Monastic Research.

Choose a whole number of research monks and Apply allocation. Reassignment is
free and uses no major action. The slider previews medical capacity and research;
the confirmed assignment begins the next calendar day. Other monks tend patients.
New recruits begin in the infirmary. Losses clamp allocations to surviving monks.

Each research monk adds 1 progress per calendar day. Every 100 progress produces
1 Secret. Progress is retained while paused; overflow and multiple discoveries
are preserved. A month-long preparation turn processes each actual calendar day,
including recruitment and allocation dates, and combines discoveries in its report.
The progress bar, current/projected discovery estimates and pending assignment
appear together. The Technology summary reads this same saved state.

Existing patients remain when staffing falls. Treatment capacity falls with the
number of infirmary monks: excess patients wait without losing prior treatment.
Patients are not evicted. Moving monks away therefore has a medical consequence.
Old archives initialise with all monks in the infirmary and no retroactive research.

DETAILS AND REVIEW

  Integrated mechanics 36.11.txt - rules and links between systems.
  Mechanics review queue 36.11.txt - eight outstanding design/balance questions.

Research intentionally keeps the requested 100-progress threshold and 1 progress
per monk/day. At 100 researchers, all 33 Secrets required by the current fourteen
technology catalogue can be earned in 33 days, before considering other spending.
This is a balance issue for review, not an undisclosed change to the approved rate.
Active fleet defeats still do not generate damaged hulls; the new repair queue
handles existing damaged hulls. The review queue distinguishes this and other
remaining event-integration issues from implemented mechanics.
