WAR THEATRE CARTOGRAPHY — NOVEMBER 2026

The base geographical painting is unchanged. All changes are vector overlays
registered to that painting. Coastlines are traced from the illustration;
inland frontiers use interpreted ridges and river corridors, not survey data.
The 11 province IDs and their save mappings remain unchanged.

Marked areas corrected
- Eastern Adriatic: a narrow Venetian Dalmatian coastal strip; Croatian lands
  grouped with Hungary; Bosnia and Ragusa shown separately as neutral context.
  Kotor, Zara, Spalato and Sebenico are Venetian ports. Venetian Adriatic island outlines are grouped with their metropolis. Existing Istria remains
  in the Venetian mainland outline. Small port markers do not claim hinterland.
- Western Greece: Ottoman Epirus and the Thessalian coastal gap now form part
  of the existing Macedonian operational district. Chalkidiki's three fingers
  follow the coastline. No Mount Athos label has been introduced.
- Corfu retains Venetian affiliation. Cephalonia, Leucas and Zakynthos form
  contextual Tocco lordships, rather than being falsely coloured Venetian in
  1453. They do not create three separate diplomatic relationships.
- Opsikion and Thrakesion now include their omitted western promontories;
  Genoese-associated Lesbos and Chios remain distinct islands.
- The Antalya coastal gap belongs to the Cibyrrhaeot operational district.
  Karaman and that district meet along an irregular shared Taurus frontier.
- Long Karaman/Anatolikon, Armeniakon and Georgian borders were redrawn around
  foothill and river corridors. Naples' artificial stepped frontier was removed.
- Genoese Corsica added; South Crimea retains the Genoese group, with the
  overlapping Gazaria headline removed (colony markers remain).
- Aq Qoyunlu added near the upper Tigris / Diyarbakir. This is a deliberately
  restrained 1453 extent, not the much larger Iranian empire of the late 1460s.

Affiliation is a gameplay grouping. Associated Gattilusio and maritime
lordships retain explanatory notes; Genoese association is not a claim that
every local institution was administered identically. Paphlagonia remains
fully Ottoman as explicitly requested earlier for this prototype.

Validation
67 unique region IDs; exactly 11 Ottoman province IDs; normalised coordinates
within bounds; no duplicate vertices; no strict edge self-intersections.
Ownership masks are partitioned before tracing. Touching edges and very small
coastal painterly inaccuracies are possible at this illustration's resolution.
Generated preview visually reviewed. Unity interaction checks are separate.

Reproducibility
map_cartography_revision.py stages a new JSON from the captured baseline and
reuses the original coastline classifier/tracer in wbw33_coastlines.py. It
also emits a diagnostic overlay preview. It never writes the base artwork.
