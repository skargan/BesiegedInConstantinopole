CONTRACTS
ProvincialIntelligence.Advance(DateTime): resolves due arrival/operation/recall once and returns readable report. GetSupplyPressure(): read-only queued delivery reduction. ImperialSupply.Deliver(int,out string): consumes queued reduction once per actual delivery, returns remaining shipment. Caller owns delivery scheduling and setting camp stocks. Empty delivery does not consume disruption; positive delivery retains at least one store-day. Do not call for forecasts.
Existing dictionaries store iWbw* fields. Operation success/loss is rolled and saved when ordered, not when report opened. iSpies remains agents in enemy camp; dispatch subtracts one, recall returns one, death never subtracts a second camp agent.
