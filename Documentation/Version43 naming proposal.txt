INTERNAL RESEARCH NOTES
- Latin is an original diplomatic/chancery language, not always the local spoken
  language. The selector should be called Original names, not Native languages.
- Venice: Treccani dates the official Maggior Consiglio formula Serenissima
  signoria to 1462. Comune Veneciarum is the stricter proposal for 1453. Most
  Serene Republic of Venice is an alternative familiar historical-fiction title.
- Lithuania and Poland remain two realms under one monarch, NOT the post-1569
  Commonwealth. Latin preserves a shared period documentary language without
  inventing a single Lithuanian-Polish state name. A bilingual Lithuanian/Polish
  alternative is possible, with normalised spelling explicitly recorded here.
- Papacy/Holy See is the diplomatic court. Papal States remains the short map
  territory label. No map-label replacement is implied.
- Naples: archival distinction citra/ultra Pharum separates mainland and island
  Sicily. Do not rename all possessions or imply a unitary Kingdom of Two Sicilies.
- Albania and Po Lordships are alliance/aggregate labels, not unitary kingdoms.
- Original-language proposals for Albania, Wallachia, Moldavia and Serbia are
  normalised labels, NOT verified transcriptions of 1453 chancery documents.
- Trebizond/Morea are descriptive Greek renderings; do not claim a unique attested
  legal state title. Theodoro's long form derives from recorded ruler titulature.
- Crimean and Candar Arabic-script spellings require a specialist transcription
  check before being presented as exact period spelling. Karaman and Ottoman
  originals deliberately use shorter regional/dynastic labels.
- Mamluk Circassian-dynasty label is a period-oriented alternative to the modern
  conventional Mamluk Sultanate, not an exclusive legal title.
- Gaza/Caffa display identity must not imply independence from Genoa or change
  existing Bank relationships. All dependencies retain their grouped identities.
