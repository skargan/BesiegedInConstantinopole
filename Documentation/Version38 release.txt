BESIEGED IN CONSTANTINOPLE — VERSION 38
Provisions, hunger and menu polish | 4 November 2026

CHANGES
- Constantinople and the Ottoman camp now use physical provisions: one provision feeds 1,000 people for a day. Fractional quantities are preserved.
- Provincial food reserves are finite. Destructive Wealth loss destroys the same share of remaining undispatched food; Wealth recovery does not replace it.
- Fortnightly convoys draw from actual reserves at departure. Later damage cannot remove cargo already travelling. Interception transfers or destroys that cargo once.
- Summer harvest replenishes reserves up to editable caps; winter shipping can continue from stored food.
- Consecutive city shortages escalate public morale losses, with half-rate rounded-down penalties to army, clergy and nobles. Partial shortages scale the losses.
- Ottoman hunger causes escalating, proportional desertion across land troops. Five entirely unfed days lose 1,500 men; surviving strength determines future demand.
- A fully supplied day resets escalation. Fractional stocks, convoy cargo, shortage counters and desertion remainders survive saves.
- Infirmary treatment costs one extra provision per 20 patients actually treated, in addition to ordinary city rations. Food and monk availability limit treatment.
- Food reports respect intelligence levels. Fixed an earlier-save restore issue that could retain later shortage/cargo data.
- Top statistics have centred labels and icon/value pairs, consistent number sizes and clearer grouping.
- Begin your reign has three vertically ordered choices: Normal campaign, Start at the Siege, Ironman. Explanations appear on hover; replay heading and note are centred.
- Updated AGENT COORDINATION instructions, design decisions and release record.
