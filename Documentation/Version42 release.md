# Version 42 — The Living City

**Against Fate: Saving Constantinople** — Windows playtest, 6 November 2026.

## Changes since Version 41

- **Decision Journal:** separate Daily Notes, Decisions and Strategic Status tabs. The strategic dashboard follows the actual Bosphorus passage, Golden Horn chain, formal Galata alignment, Union of Florence and naval dominance; the Turkish battalion appears once formed. Existing journal records and save behaviour remain in use.
- **Regional map:** Malta's mercenary marketplace and departure point now sit on the island south of Sicily. Candar has a distinct Sinop coastal territory. Eleven provincial History pages explain the regions and their past. Earlier annotated coastal corrections were reconciled with the current map and the original reference was preserved.
- **Court religion:** renamed the Foreign powers view to Non-Ottoman Powers. Fixed Catholic colouring for Lithuania, Gazaria, Cyprus, Athens, the Ionian lordships and Bosnia. Qualified narrative labels no longer incorrectly appear as Unclassified; all current regions have mappings. This layer describes ruling courts, not population faith.
- **Technology discoveries:** hand-traced hover/click outlines for the skull, pigeon letter, chest catch and six alchemy objects. A compact apparatus keeps every alchemy step visible beside the default menu. Existing once-per-campaign rewards and independent discoveries are preserved. Music and sound effects remain On by default for fresh installations.
- **Military and shipyard:** corrected recruitment dialogs that appeared behind the menu, added distinct training artwork and a dedicated shipyard construction screen, with side-by-side archer firing lanes. Added concise descriptions to strategic focus choices.
- **Fleet orders:** defensive Golden Horn orders are the default, supporting wall defence without routine reports. Revised volunteer and plunder rewards, supply raids drawn from actual Ottoman stocks, and closed-straits gating for Crimean grain. Monastic research requires 1,000 progress for one secret. Naval preparation bonuses remain explicitly under development and do not modify core rewards.
- **The Senate and intelligence:** traced delegation outlines, a separate Galatan hand/bribery shortcut, painted research/shipyard/Hagia Sophia navigation, and a compact framed intelligence header with hover explanations.
- **Court life:** Kjold's biography, security assessment and Guard history, followed by reviewed court orders with persisted service consequences. No assassination-plot subsystem was introduced. Emperor's chamber artwork and mirror/journal links repaired.
- **Ragusa:** detailed negotiations and the current representative require a resident envoy; concluded agreements continue after recall. The main Return button leads back to War Beyond the Walls.
- **Theodoro:** approved imperial-ties, archery-cooperation and technology-linked cavalry agreements are playable; reincorporation remains deferred.
- **Atmosphere and writing:** layered council windows, smoother cloud and painted-ship movement, gilded quotation presentation and icon-only header; smoother Nameless Fortress vulture flight; 61 approved Grain Merchant remarks. Copa begins as an undeveloped anchorage and gains its established compound view later.

## How to play

Extract the complete folder and run **Saving Constantinople.exe**. Keep the Data folder and supporting libraries alongside it. The version is visible in the game. Saves are written to the local game folder and can be copied for diagnosis/export. Start a fresh campaign; older-save compatibility is not supported for this playtest.

## Verification and limits

The release uses the same combined source verified in focused exported-player checks. A fresh Version42 Windows export runs editor save validation; final-build journal, court-map, discovery and diplomatic checks are recorded in the publication manifest. Earlier focused checks also cover recruitment, fleet orders, Senate navigation, provincial histories, vulture motion, Ragusa and Kjold. These checks are not a claim of complete long-campaign balance.

Review-only Notaras/Giustiniani dialogue, Theodoro reincorporation, Bank takeover routes and other explicitly deferred designs are not newly activated by this release. No personal saves, test captures or editor/debug symbol folders are included in the downloadable package.
