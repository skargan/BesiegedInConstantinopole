BESIEGED VERSION36.8 — SOCIETY AND COURT

Society and politics now opens the five civic measures directly: army morale, public morale, noble support, clergy support, Galata relations. Existing audiences and their effects remain.
Retired faction influence, passives, drift, weekly favours, political debts, faction banishment and allegiance-based character exclusion. Old saves neutralise these keys; already experienced consequences and queued recruitment dates remain historical state.
Removed merchant favour, personal fencing progression and general mentorship statistics. The daily merchant levy remains. The Apprentice Gunner is kept as its particular event, including the five-day outcome.
The palace map opens Defence of the walls. The old Imperial Affairs panel and its duplicate garden/rest/lead-next-assault controls are retired. The palace garden, assault decisions and sword minigame remain accessible.
Siege overview now shows the current commander's benefit and Change commander. A serving adviser can be selected, reviewed and confirmed. Final appointment uses the hourglass and cannot double-submit. The outgoing adviser keeps their ordinary specialist contribution while in service.
