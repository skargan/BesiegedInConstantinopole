KJOLD CONVERSATIONS AND COURT ORDERS
6 November 2026 — JohanX

User-approved character canon
Kjold was born and raised in Constantinople. His father came from Gotland,
his mother from Crete. He visited the north once with his father and retains
a strange nostalgia for it. The later correction from Morea to Crete is explicit.
Authorised prose is held in Resources/Content/KjoldDialogue.json.

Conversation
Palace/Garden -> existing Kjold figure -> dedicated three-topic conversation.
Tell me about yourself; How do you assess my security; Tell me about the
Varangian Guard. Text can scroll independently beside the portrait; topics
remain visible. The Guard account has four paragraphs. The first two draw on
the historical Guard; its continued 1452 organisation and thirty homelands,
including two Tatars and a Finn, are the game's fictional continuation.

Security ALWAYS opens with no known assassination plots, per the user's
explicit correction. No assassination-plot system or discovery branch added.
Enemy spies: unknown below eight camp agents, exact current iTurkSpies at
eight or more, matching the existing intelligence gate. No random roll.
First reading stores iKjoldSecurityHeard and unlocks I have orders.

Orders
Exile / Imprison / Execute -> serving-courtier list -> sentence review ->
Confirm or Cancel. Eligible: core court officers, the current arrived visiting
adviser, and Shaherezade while serving as storyteller or intelligence contact.
Kjold does not sentence himself through his own interface. Absent/dead,
unrecruited and already-sentenced people are excluded from the target list.

Order states: iCourtSentence{person}: 0 none, 1 exile, 2 prison, 3 execution.
Core characters also reuse iCourtState: exile 6, prison 4, dead 3, and their
existing service bool is removed. Visitors cease service and cannot re-arrive
from their old invitation. Shaherezade leaves her service state. Sentencing
the active commander returns command to the Emperor (iCommander -1).
Service bonuses, command eligibility and conversations stop. Court cards
remain visible with EXILED / IMPRISONED / EXECUTED stamps, and the decision
journal records the sentence. Other inactive characters remain hidden.

No extra gold cost, major-action cost, loyalty penalty, constituency penalty
or random outcome was invented. These balance terms remain undesigned.
Prisoner release, pardon, trials and later prisoner conversations are not
implemented; they can be developed separately. Confirmation states this.
Existing completed rewards are not retrospectively removed.

Persistence
Unlock and sentences are standard campaign integers. A narrow
KjoldCourtOrders.ResetForRestore hook clears them before restoring a saved
snapshot, so an earlier save without these keys does not inherit a later
sentence. New campaigns use the existing dictionary reset.
