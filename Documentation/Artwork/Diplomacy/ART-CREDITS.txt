Aq Qoyunlu banner: artistic reconstruction, generated 3 November 2026. Black Bayandur dynastic tamga reproduced from Wikimedia Commons, Tamga of Bayandur (Aq Qoyunlu version), attributed to V. Minorsky; uploaded by Beshogur. Source cites Minorsky (1939), A Soyurghal of Qasim b. Jahangir Aq-qoyunlu (903/1498), BSOAS 9(4), 927-960. Emblem is attested on coins; ivory cloth, gold edging and fabric treatment are artistic reconstruction, not an authenticated surviving 1452 flag.
