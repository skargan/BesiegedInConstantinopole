VERSION 36.3 - Council quotations
1 November 2026

This combined release includes the compact intelligence desk from Version36.2 and the completed Imperial Council quotation system.

The Council shows a different quotation each campaign turn, with author/speaker and a short source. The catalogue contains392 entries from the designer's edited document. A campaign has its own stable order: reopening the Council or loading the same turn preserves its quotation. Quote selection does not use gameplay randomness or alter campaign resources. Save format is unchanged.

Version36.2 was exported before the quotation implementation was complete; it therefore retains the older fixed quotation. Version36.3 includes the implemented and tested catalogue and selector.

All prior Version36 updates remain: compact preparation, campaign/settings/wall defence screens; revised cursor; recruitment logistics and manpower limit; assassination action; Hagia Sophia entrances; Sultan audience layout; Reports/Operations intelligence tabs.

Use the Besieged-Latest desktop shortcut to open this combined release. Version-specific shortcuts continue to open their labelled versions. Saves are carried forward from the latest saved copy in the previous Version36 build folders; older folders remain intact.
