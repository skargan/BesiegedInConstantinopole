FIXED
1. Reputation and other percentage fractions could exceed 100%. Raw changes and checkpoint restoration now clamp them. Equipment quality keeps its independent lethality multiplier scale; 1.2 is legitimate quality, not 120% civic support.
2. City provisions never fell with daily population consumption. Food now decreases once per completed siege day; preparation weeks consume no siege rations. The hunger state is saved.
3. Treasury and market navigation was missing. The council now offers provisions, materials and ammunition with quantity controls, total costs and affordability checks. Documented prices: provisions 2 gold before the siege / 10 during it; materials 10; ammunition 50. Emergency levy: 100 gold per population unit, public morale -15 points, major action.
4. Empty stores now produce a recoverable shortage encounter after the normal event. Emergency purchase supplies three days; private requisition supplies two days with noble support -5; reserve rations supply one day with army morale -5 / public morale +2; enduring hunger costs public morale -3 / army morale -2. These shortage effects are provisional values. Buying in the market can avert the encounter.
5. Five advertised charges were absent: Orban ordinary hire 20,000 gold; focus hire 10,000; fortress sabotage 3,000; Grand Cannon 5,000; the counterbattery workshop outcome stated a 3,000 cost. All now deduct their price once. Workshop eligibility and choice text now show its expense before commitment. Galata choices show bribe and troop/ship/secret costs.
6. Ending detection could be followed by an arrival report. Management now leaves the ending modal intact when a daily report is pending.
7. Fleet orders and Grand Cannon possession now use named states in outcome reports. Equipment quality is reported as a lethality multiplier, and a vacant command is reported correctly.

BALANCE AND REMAINING GAPS
Nine of ten final campaigns survived 240 days. One War Economy campaign lost during preparations when noble support reached zero. This is substantially more forgiving than the intended rare victory rate; bug-free completion does not establish satisfactory balance.
Cheap repeated repairs, strong early reinforcements, repeatable emergency levies and requisitions, and a limited Ottoman pressure model merit a dedicated balance pass. The emergency grain sources currently pay a political/morale cost rather than drawing from finite private inventories. A reserve/debt model would make that trade-off more durable.
The Ottoman AI still cannot use all naval and Grand Cannon ownership flags as distinct threats. The Grand Cannon and harbour-chain variables can be set by events but do not have a complete dedicated combat/action loop. These are feature gaps, not resolved by the editorial or economy pass.
The current zero-support ending remains immediate, including during preparation. The earlier recoverable political-crisis direction requires reconciliation with those ending rules. Hunger itself is recoverable; zero provisions alone is not an ending.
Preparation still uses the existing weekly calendar. Monthly preparation, fuller Genoese politics and richer negotiated endings remain further design work.
The next useful balance experiment is to make repeated defence and emergency funding create cumulative costs while letting Ottoman pressure escalate. This report does not silently alter assault probabilities or existing victory/defeat rules.
