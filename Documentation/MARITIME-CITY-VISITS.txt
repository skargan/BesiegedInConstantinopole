GENOA AND VENICE — CITY LANDMARK VISITS
5 November 2026; staged after the Version 40 release commit.

Each city has its own existing-style circular city landmark. Click it to open a
wide historical painting and a shallow lower panel with a brief description,
Diplomacy with Genoa/Venice, and Return to the map. Back/Escape closes the visit
before the map. The diplomacy button opens the existing country's court.
No new goodwill, aid, travel, gold, event or time rules were added.

Artwork is an atmospheric reconstruction in the game's established painterly
palette, generated through built-in image_gen. Both cities were composed with
quiet water below so the panel does not cover the main architecture. Genoa uses
steep Ligurian hills and a crowded medieval harbour; Venice uses the Gothic
Doge's Palace and St Mark's waterfront. These are not documentary reconstructions
of every building or rigging detail in 1452.

Artwork resource paths:
