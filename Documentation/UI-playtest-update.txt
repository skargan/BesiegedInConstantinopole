UI PLAYTEST UPDATE - 12 OCTOBER 2026

Changed
- Persistent Return control appears whenever a submenu can be left; campaign menu remains available during turn phases without undoing committed choices.
- Recruitment now closes its overlay correctly, and returning reaches management.
- Conscription divides troops by population in people. Initial 100 population units = 100,000 people; initial 5,200 counted troops = 5.2%.
- Resource cards replace the plain status text, with full population and grouped numeric values.
- Larger management buttons, readable sans-serif text, enlarged event layout, mouse continuation and explicit recruitment/training labels.
- Repair preview shows cost, expected percentage-point gain, final integrity range and major-action usage.
- Five vector focus emblems with distinct colours and text labels identify initial focus choices and focus-gated event choices.
- Notaras focus warning, Schism wording and wall-description wording updated. Bracketed system text uses contrasting colour and italic style; redundant Explanation labels removed.
- Orban imprisonment option exposes 60% escape / 35% imprisonment / 5% fatal explosion. Fatal branch now applies death rather than only describing it. Rejection exposes 85% joining Mehmed / 15% execution.

Validation
- Unity scene update completed.
- Expanded gameplay checks cover population display, two-soldier recruitment, exact payment, no recruitment turn cost, overlay return, repair effects, 27 turn transitions, loss and restart.
- New Windows output is Builds/Windows-Playtest2, keeping the running older output intact.

Scope
- Training remains unavailable; labelled accordingly rather than implying a working action.
- Focus changes remain unimplemented, clearly disclosed at the initial focus decision.
- No new balance formula or historical population estimate is imposed; 100,000 is the designer's chosen starting population.

Event playtest additions
- Faith and Unity receives ONLY the approved wartime compact. Orthodoxy stays, Isidore remains, clergy support falls 5 points, full-Union aid is not unlocked. The persuasion alternative was not added.
- Partial Union retains Isidore; the exile choice explicitly warns that he becomes unavailable.
- Completed events automatically show the selected decision, actual resource/troop changes, new totals and character availability. Population uses people, not thousands, in reports.
- Event choices have individual text-based heights in a scrollable panel. Focus names use one shared vocabulary.
- Arrival of the Genoese adds 500 infantry and 200 archers when accepted. This 700-man gameplay split is provisional, not a historical unit-type claim.
- Four previously empty headings now contain small DRAFT choices: Imperial Wardrobe (4000/6000 gold sale, noble support -5); Mind over Matter (Grant costs 3000 gold, +10 engineers, chance of +40 materials); Venetians (2500 gold for +200 infantry, +2 ships and Diedo); Our Turks (+300 infantry and Orhan, noble support -5).
- Sultan's Envoy also receives draft refusal responses instead of an empty event.
- Wardrobe sale and Grant's discovery provide limited preparation variation. A full random-event pool and shuffled preparation are not implemented.
- Quick start unlocks on reaching the siege and repeats the last preparation checkpoint, including decisions and resources. It does not invent an opaque preset for first-time players.
- Siege begins at story23, on 6 April 1453. Galata no longer triggers premature Ottoman attacks. 240-day victory counts completed siege days.
- Assault reports replace placeholder titles/descriptions with the enemy action; non-assault days skip the misleading casualty/funeral screen.
