VERSION32 — OTTOMAN INTENTIONS —25 OCTOBER2026

Player-visible changes
The Ottoman commander begins with Rapid Assault. Four persistent personalities—Rash,Aggressive,Cautious,Balanced—bias the same four strategic policies. Rapid Assault plans a14–28day campaign;Methodical Assault30–60;Golden Horn andStrangulation21–42. Each has a buildup phase; later regrouping can last up to30days. A costly assault or successful advance may trigger an early review. Strategy review can choose the same strategy again. These are provisional gameplay timings, not historical claims.

Rapid Assault:reinforce the initial artillery,fill the moat,bombard,then assault. Methodical Assault:ramps,towers,rams,mantlets and deliberate waves. Golden Horn:overland slipways or chain-tower bombardment,then coastal pressure. Strangulation:infiltrators,store sabotage,carcass bombardment,naval pressure and Galatan opportunities. Operations are selected from weighted,validated data in Resources/Content/OttomanStrategies.json. Invalid/null/empty tables fail validation; legal alternatives have a reconnaissance fallback. Legacy scene tactic tables are retained for compatibility but no longer select the turn's operation.

One planned operation is sampled and saved before the player's council action. Reading reports never resamples it. Actual execution is once per turn, including enemy-food consumption and delayed turns. Known resupply and delay turns are forecast explicitly. Sabotage or changed circumstances may invalidate an operation and force a fallback. A destroyed wall explicitly permits advancing to the next line. Save/resume retains strategy,personality,phase,estimates,plan and operation stamp. Existing older archives initialise these fields when necessary.

Intelligence network
1enemy manpower;2artillery/fleet (including great bombard presence);3enemy stores;4consumption;5fury;6morale;7stored coming operation;8enemy infiltrators AND their estimate of main-wall strength,wall integrity,coastal strength and confidence;9strategy/phase/personality;10reserved placeholder. Ottoman estimates persist,drift towards reports with bounded error,and improve with infiltrators and combat. Tactical weighting uses estimates rather than exact defender strength. Intelligence reads themselves have no random or gameplay effects.

The baseline mechanics document and starting data give5Ottoman cannons;this starting count is preserved. Reinforcement operations add1cannon,8ammunition and150irregulars. A later heavy-convoy operation can deploy the great bombard,adding3percentage points to wall bombardment. These new reinforcement and heavy-gun values are provisional. Ottoman infiltration attempts are a visible operation with a hidden45percent success roll,capped at10infiltrators. The result is not disclosed as a precise spy count below the relevant intelligence level.

Arrow shields(mantlets) reduce incoming arrow damage25percent while intact;they confer no cannon immunity. A gun-target choice lets the defender shatter them:half the existing gun allocation's casualty output,then full arrow effect for subsequent volleys. This is available as an untimed decision even with minigames off. The assault result freezes remaining screens so campaign commit uses the same result as the ledger. Dedicated mantlet animation/art remains future work.

Golden Horn access now has two actionable routes:progressive overland works or progressive destruction of the chain anchorage. Coast attacks are announced one council day ahead and resolved by the existing sea-wall garrison system. The Ottoman director owns new warning scheduling;the old independent automatic warning scheduler is disabled when the new director is active. Access does not instantly end a campaign. Separately moving fleets between Marmara and Golden Horn,distinct naval-control pools and a generated geopolitical map remain future work. Horn ship metadata is a subset of the existing Ottoman fleet,not newly spawned vessels.

Fleet and supply
Routine fleet results are outside the narrative-event lottery. Each cycle samples14–21active siege days and saves the interval;reading the remaining time does not reroll it. Changed orders begin a fresh cycle. Effects/reward amounts are retained from the current prototype,so the increased frequency needs balance review. Daily Ottoman ship growth remains1per siege day;source metadata alternates Gallipoli and Izmit. Province capture/shipyard interruption and wider imperial production are future systems,not yet simulated by that metadata.

The supplied consolidation also fixes commander preparation to award actual army morale once,prioritises fixed milestones over due narrative chapters,distinguishes land/sea relief routes,limits storms to sea transport,and removes implied free warships from ordinary transports. Surrender pressure accounts for defensive depth. See consolidation notes for the detailed previously approved scope.

First provincial slice
War Beyond the Walls under Spies provides one Thessaloniki agent cell. Dispatch subtracts a camp agent;travel,investigation/sabotage,recall and results persist. Specialists shows existing workforce and assignments. Successful depot sabotage queues a bounded reduction in the next imperial convoy. ImperialSupply.Deliver is the sole delivery adapter;CouncilActions.ResolveEnemySupplies remains the sole consumption tick. It consumes each queued disruption once and reports actual supplies. Wider rebellions,foreign fronts,Morea/Athos diplomacy,resident envoys and medical recovery remain documented backlog.

Presentation
Settings uses an independent top-level canvas to avoid clipping/interception after a resolution change. Fullscreen and Windowed buttons remain available;F11 andAlt+Enter are additional switching controls. Both directions retain preferences. Repeated pointer-hit tests are recorded in the runtime log. Short official biographies now identify palace characters without revealing deeper personal information;the earlier longer passages remain in source for further conversations.
