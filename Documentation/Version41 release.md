# Version 41 — Courts of the Black Sea and Adriatic

Against Fate: Saving Constantinople — Windows playtest, 5 November 2026.

## Playable additions
- **Candar / Sinop:** a distinct court, guarded and open background scenes, Ismail Bey's conversations, and secret negotiations opened through Orhan while the Bosphorus is open. Ransom captives, purchase three finite secrets, or delay an eligible Ottoman land operation. Confirm every payment before committing. Paphlagonia remains an Ottoman province.
- **Ragusa:** a dedicated court with alternating representatives, separate personal favour, monthly private food shipments, and paid service abroad for an existing troop contingent. Survivors return through recruitment logistics; troops abroad remain reserved and stop consuming city provisions while away.
- **Genoese Gazaria / Caffa:** a separate diplomatic relationship from Genoa, a harbour encounter with the consul and a Bank of St George representative, and access to existing embassy relations. Corrected an old alias that had redirected Gazaria to Genoa.
- **Copa, the Circassian coast:** a map-only port encounter, an imperial trading compound, a choice of weekly gold or food consignments, finite food stocks, sea-voyage risks and a locally negotiated mercenary household using the existing contract and arrival system.
- **Theodoro / Mangup:** a dedicated illustrated encounter with realm, imperial ties and archery conversations, preserving existing aid and envoy actions.
- Includes the completed Genoa and Venice visit presentation from the parallel workstream.

## Finding the content
Open Diplomacy and court, then Diplomatic relations for Ragusa, Gazaria and Theodoro. Candar can also be reached through the Sinop marker. Copa is reached only through its marker on the eastern Black Sea coast in War Beyond the Walls.

## Balance and unfinished content
Copa's provisional trading terms are 5,000 gold to establish, then 350 gold OR 100 provisions each week, with a 14-day baseline voyage; the exportable food reserve starts at 2,400. The designer has been asked to confirm the food quantity. Theodoro's numerical archery/cavalry agreements require final terms; its new encounter is narrative only. Theodoro reincorporation and the mercenary hub, and Bank takeover routes, remain deferred. This is not a claim that those deferred systems are playable.

## Verification
Final Unity Windows build and editor save validation passed. 169 exported-player diplomatic checks cover dealings, gating, finite rewards, saved state, troop deployment/returns, finite convoy departure/delivery, route closure, separate court identities and screen layout/art at 1280x720 and 1920x1080. The existing 275-check mercenary regression also passed. Screenshots were reviewed for the five destinations. These focused checks do not establish long-campaign balance.

Fresh campaigns recommended; older save compatibility is not supported. No personal saves or test captures are included in the package.
