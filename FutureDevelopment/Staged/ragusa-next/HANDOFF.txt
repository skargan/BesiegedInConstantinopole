IMPLEMENTED RULES
Gazulus chairs January 1452; Cotrugli February; alternate each calendar month.
Their personal favour is stored separately, initially 50. National goodwill stays
in CountryDiplomacy. Personal favour is tracked and shown but does not yet alter
prices, probabilities or unlock new dialogue. Do not describe those future effects
as implemented.

Food: 2,500 gold buys 500 provisions; 14-day sea journey; once per calendar month,
one private shipment pending at a time. Official refusal accompanies a committed
purchase: no acceptance roll. Existing storms can delay or destroy the cargo.
The transport does not join the player's fleet. Price and transit are editable
playtest choices, not historical measurements.

War: commit 500 infantry, 500 archers and 200 cavalry immediately. Receive 5,000
gold, +5 Ragusan goodwill and +5 favour with the signing representative. Resolve
after 90 calendar days with a single 50/50 roll. Victory loses 1–20% independently
per troop category, pays another 10,000 gold and grants +10 goodwill/+10 signing
representative favour. Defeat loses 1–50% independently per category. Survivors
return 14 days after resolution through the existing recruitment arrival list.
Only one contract at a time. Departing troops are deployment, not casualties;
only actual losses enter casualty records. Deployed/returning men remain reserved.
Signing either agreement uses the existing major-action flow; inspection and
cancellation are free. Confirmation shows the full cost and commitment first.
