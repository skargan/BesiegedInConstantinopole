RULES
Open secret negotiations requires bStraitsOpen and Orhan in service. Both are
shown even on the disabled button. They are opening conditions; an established
understanding does not require Orhan to remain in service. No claim or rebellion
is started, and Candar has no ordinary envoy, aid or alliance controls.

Ransom: 3,000 gold, one convoy per seven calendar days. Independent inclusive
rolls: infantry 20–60, archers 15–30 and total population 150–300 guaranteed;
cavalry, engineers and monks each 50% for 1–5; envoy 5% for one; guards 10% for 1–3.
The total population includes those entering service. One payment and one combined
arrival report. The seven-day limit follows the adopted existing implementation.

Secrets: 2,000 gold, one immediate secret, three distinct named purchases per
campaign. Failed purchases spend nothing and do not consume the remaining count.

Disruption: 2,500 gold, one eligible land operation deferred by one calendar day,
seven-day purchase cooldown, no overlapping unconsumed credit. The calendar,
supplies and naval pressure continue. Current emergency priorities remain ahead
of deferred orders; invalid obsolete orders are discarded. The next available
date is visible. Transactions retain the earlier zero-turn-cost rule and now have
a terms review before payment; cancelling is free.
