CORRECTIONS
1. The old deferred-order hook ran before every normal planner priority. It could force a deferred bombardment despite a fallen wall or new supply emergency. The new planner clause follows all three critical priorities and keeps the current SharedProvisions.Camp/OttomanDemand logic intact.
2. A deferred operation is checked for a valid enum, eligibility and present legality before reuse. A completed moat or other obsolete target no longer forces an invalid action; its already-used postponement is cleared.
3. NavalAssault, OverlandShips and ChainBattery are excluded from the paid land disruption. So are Resupply, AwaitOrders, Reinforce and mandatory Advance. Other tactical land operations can be held for one calendar day. Daily calendar, supply resolution and the separate naval systems are untouched.
4. Pending deferrals are not silently consumed by emergency or naval operations. A due valid order resumes after an intervening mandatory emergency permits it; the purchase does not add another delay day.

EXACT RULES
Opening requires bStraitsOpen and CourtRoster.InService(3), the existing Prince Orhan slot. Opening changes only iDiplomacyCandarUnlocked, with no claim, rebellion, ordinary envoy, aid or alliance. Requirements apply to opening negotiations, as briefed; no invented later transaction gate.
Ransom:3,000 gold; once every7 calendar days. Every row draws independently. Infantry20–60, archers15–30 and total population150–300 always arrive. Cavalry, engineers and monks each independently50percent for1–5. Envoy5percent for1. Guards10percent for1–3. All ranges inclusive. The total population already includes every volunteer; it is not added again for military/specialist entries. Only specialists/envoy/guards are additionally committed in SpecialistPersonnel because regular troops already appear in serving manpower.
Secrets:2,000 gold each; three distinct finite pieces, chosen without replacement, each adds1 available secret. No payment/RNG when unavailable.
Disruption:2,500 gold,7-day cooldown and only one unconsumed purchase. It postpones one eligible land tactical order until the next calendar day. No calendar pause, supply pause or sea-system pause. Resolved attacks are not reversed.
All changes use existing iDiplomacyCandar... campaign integers. No transient purchase flags or independent random generator.
