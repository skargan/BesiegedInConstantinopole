4 November 2026. Original fictional dialogue for Besieged.
Preserved verbatim from the user's revised list; no silent rewriting.

1. “You expect another spring, Emperor? So do the leaves.”

2. “Mehmed wants your city. I worry about what he will want afterwards.”

3. “Your walls are impressive. Your creditors speak highly of them.”

4. “I prefer an emperor across the water to a sultan at my gate.”

5. “Let us conduct business before either of us becomes history.”

6. “Rome has fallen so many times... You have becoming exceedingly efficient at it.”

7. “A secret is merely a fact whose price has not yet been agreed.”

8. “Of course I am loyal to the Sultan. We are discussing commerce.”

9. “Do not mistake my discretion for affection.”

10. “Mehmed is young. He has not yet learned to leave anything unconquered.”

11. “I have sent him assurances. They travel more cheaply than soldiers.”

12. “You have very little land for a man with so many titles.”

13. “My merchants favour peace. Their finest profits suggest otherwise.”

14. “A captive has a price. Ask him what freedom is worth.”

15. “Your gold bears a reassuring face. I hope you remain available to mint it.”

16. “The sea connects us. Fortunately, it also separates us.”

17. “I hear your court is divided. At least there is still something to divide.”

18. “Orhan has an excellent claim. Claims are safest in somebody else’s house.”

19. “I would offer advice freely, but you might undervalue it.”

20. “The Sultan calls it destiny. His quartermasters use other words.”

21. “One misplaced instruction can accomplish what a hundred brave men cannot.”

22. “You need another day. How fortunate that I know men who waste them.”

23. “I do not oppose great empires. I merely prefer to stand outside them.”

24. “Your Roman ancestors understood strength. How do you feel about being a shadow of their shadow?”

25. “A locked gate is discourteous. An unguarded one is expensive.”

26. “Keep your promises modest, Emperor. You may survive to fulfil them.”

27. “Every conqueror loves his own independence, but it annoys him in others.”

28. “Mehmet may want to put me under his boot, but only after he gets you.”

29. “I hope you win. Quietly, of course.”

30. “Do come again. It would disappoint me to learn our last transaction was charitable.”
