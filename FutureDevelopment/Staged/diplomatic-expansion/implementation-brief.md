Status: design staging only; shared checkout frozen for ConstantineX consolidation.

Circassian coast: map-only port, no diplomacy-list entry or additional territory. Candidate Copa near Kuban mouth; confirm exact placement. Merchant contract price, troops and travel duration require balancing. Emperor does not physically travel in these screens.

Malta: DEFERRED DLC, design only. The revised 4 November specification is MALTA-MERCENARIES-DLC-DESIGN.txt in this directory. It supersedes earlier Malta assumptions: regional departure ports; 50/50 cash instalments; explicit fighting-ship packages for naval companies; one persisted voyage-incident roll; occasional proposed non-cash bargains. Up to5 separate company group sprites; monthly partial offer rotation. Ordinary chartered transport ships are not awarded. No gameplay implementation yet. Malta is a fictional broker interface, not a historical mercenary-exchange claim. The separate Gazaria and Circassian outpost proposals above are not silently superseded by these Malta revisions.
