# Historical casting and encounter rules

| Encounter | Cast / identity | Evidence and fiction boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Genoese Gazaria | Borruele Grimaldi, consul | Documented consul1452–53. Use this form rather than uncritically normalising him to Bartolomeo. |
| Bank of St George | Dorino Grimaldi, creditor and political negotiator | Documented among1453 creditors participating in the Bank deliberations. His deployment to Caffa and these conversations are game fiction; do not call him the historical resident bank governor. |
| Circassian coast | Copa: the Kuban anchorage | Genoese trading settlement sharing power with Circassian lords. Frame the player action as securing an imperial trading compound within an existing port, not founding an empty land. |
| Malta | Castrum Maris, Birgu | Historical medieval sea castle. Recruiting fair and companies present together are game fiction. Avoid later Hospitaller fortress appearance. |

Bank government transfer: archive contract15November1453. Player-sponsored earlier transfer is alternate history. Gazaria remains distinct from Genoa, the Crimean Khanate and Theodoro. Government route is a one-time political settlement, not a repeatable ally-farming action. Pressure-backed transfer and financial restructuring should both preserve Genoese municipal institutions while installing Bank administration. Theodoro objects over contested coastal access and tolls; avoid automatic Theodoro war.

Named Italian captains investigated: Jacopo Piccinino and Tiberto Brandolini were active but committed in Italian warfare in1453. Do not casually place their entire historical armies in Malta. Prefer fictional small companies drawn from authentic recruitment traditions, with explicit fictional company names. Five candidate traditions: Italian mixed condotta, Ligurian crossbowmen, Swiss halberdiers/pikemen, Catalan maritime infantry, Balkan mounted retinue. Swiss are not Landsknechts. Final packages remain editable balance proposals; no new troop class is necessary.

Mercenary offers: at most5 active offers; refresh once each calendar month. Persist offerIDs, prices, troop counts, contract status and arrival dates. Signed contracts survive refresh; no reroll on reopening or loading. Recruits arrive in one sea package; transport does not add warships. Recruitment fee must exceed domestic replacement price for same composition; provisional formula1.5times total domestic unit costs rounded up to50, excluding speculative special stats. Travel duration and any blockade risk displayed before confirmation. Do not multiply payment across screen clicks.

Copa outpost:5000gold once,350gold convoy accrued per7calendar days, first departure after7days. Sea route blocked means delivery is delayed, not money teleported to treasury; connect existing convoy handling. Show pending shipments. Recruitment failure kills messenger and consumes payment, not emperor or resident envoy unless explicitly stated and agreed. Success80percent without compound,95percent with compound. RNG sampled once when order committed, stored so outcome cannot change on return/reload. Contract troop mix, fee and voyage duration to be chosen against current live balance.
