# Archive

## 1. Purpose and usage

Historical evidence is preserved for selective retrieval. Historical proposals and superseded instructions are not current requirements. Current operating state belongs in [PROJECT_COORDINATION.md](PROJECT_COORDINATION.md). Canonical archive index, 6 November 2026. Legacy files are retained as historical evidence, not active governing instructions.

## 2. Chronological decision history

The original [coordination content](Documentation/agent_coordination_content.json) preserves dated decisions and changelog entries. The migration must distinguish approved intent from speculative messages. October Isidore arrival supersedes the earlier both-clerics-at-start proposal; later implemented discovery achievements supersede their original planned status.

## 3. Superseded architectures, mechanics and workflows

Old faction, merchant-favour, separate fencing, generic engineer mentorship and archived quest prototypes are not authority to recreate these systems. Old JSON-to-Word coordination remains historical evidence with explicit archival reproduction, not the future governing format.

## 4. Completed and inactive workstreams

Original ConstantineX, JohanX and OrbanX handovers remain in the preserved records. An inactive or interrupted stream is not proof that its deliverables were integrated. Current ownership is listed only in the active coordination document.

## 5. Historical versions and releases

[PROJECT_STATUS](Documentation/PROJECT_STATUS.txt) retains older Version32/31/30/28 snapshots. These are superseded release claims. [Version43 publication](Documentation/Version43%20publication.json) records public release43; newer private builds do not replace it.

## 6. Integration failures and lessons

Preserve evidence boundaries: compile success is not runtime success; export is not publication. Recent private candidates required correction of text encoding, a reflection-test fixture, a transparent map backdrop and a generic square seal border. Final corrected combined preview passed the focused checks recorded by ConstantineX. Do not report failed candidate checks as failures of the later verified revision.

## 7. Historical communications index

[Original messages](Documentation/agent_coordination_messages.json) retain communication evidence and source references. [Extractor](Documentation/extract_coordination_messages.py) documents capture scope. Missing original OrbanX history and messages outside the captured sessions are coverage gaps. A recorded outgoing message demonstrates an attempted handover, not recipient acceptance.
